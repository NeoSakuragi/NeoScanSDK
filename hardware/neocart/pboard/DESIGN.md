# NeoCart PROG board v3 — design notes (2026-10-01)

**v3 (current):** one S29GL128P shared by ADPCM-A and ADPCM-B, like SNK's PCM chip (see "v3: shared sample flash" below).
The finished v2 (two sample flashes) is archived in `/data/backup/pboard_v2_final/`; v3 in `/data/backup/pboard_v3_final/`.

Source of truth: `design.py` (every part, pin and net; `python3 design.py` must report 0 problems).
Simulation: `sim_pboard.py` (12 checks, 1,440 strobe states for contention; mutation-tested: 10 planted bugs all caught).
Board: `gen_pboard.py` (placement + gates) → `route_pboard.py` (5 Freerouting variants in parallel, best kept) → `export_pboard.py`.

## Decisions (Bruno, 2026-09-30)
- Full-height diag-cart outline (174 × 134 mm), **2 layers**, plain **HASL** finish, finger pads exposed (no gold, no bevel).
- TSOP flash: **S29GL064N** (8 MB, TSOP-48, word mode) for P; **S29GL128P** (16 MB, TSOP-56, byte mode via BYTE#) for ADPCM-A and ADPCM-B.
  Supports the 154 of 175 MAME Neo Geo parents without protection chips (all KOF94–97, Real Bout, Last Blade, 16 MB homebrews).
- **Programmable through the gold fingers** (an external programmer plays the console) or by pre-programming the chips (XGecu T48).
- Fewest unique parts: 13 machine-placed part numbers (v1 ≈ 30). One pull value (10k), one decoupling value (100n), one bulk (10u).

## Circuit
- Every finger line that reaches a flash pin passes a 3.3 V LVC output; strobes enter 5 V-tolerant LVC/AHC inputs directly.
- **P side:** A1–A16 → U5 (16245, DIR tied) → flash A0–A15; A17 → U16 (OR with GND); A18, A19 → U17 (OR with GND).
  D0–D15 ↔ U4 (16245, DIR = R/W, channel k carries bit 15-k so the traces stay parallel).
  Flash A19–A21 = BANK0–2 OR /PORTADRS (U16): P1 at MB 7, P2 bank n at MB n (n = 0–6), 8 MB usable. Bank latch U12 (AHC273): D0–D2, clock /PORTWEL, clear /RESET.
  /OE = /ROMOE ∧ /PORTOEL ∧ /PORTOEU (U13). Transceiver enable = P read ∨ bank write ∨ programming write (U13).
- **ADPCM side:** 24-bit address captured by U6/U7 (A) and U8/U9 (B), 74LVC16374, rising edge = low bits, falling edge (U18 inverter) = high bits.
  Flash /OE = 3.3 V copy of /SDROE (/SDPOE) (U14). Data through U10/U11 74LVC8T245 (A = 3.3 V flash, B = 5 V bus).
- **Programming gates (U15, 74LVC32):** /WE = strobe OR /PROG: /PORTWEU → P, /ROMOEU → V-A, /ROMOEL → V-B. Sample buffer DIR = R/W OR /PROG.
  JP1 1-2 = PROGRAM (/PROG low), 2-3 = PLAY (R1 pulls /PROG high: no write can ever reach a flash).
- Power: slot 5 V → AMS1117-3.3. /ROMWAIT, /PWAIT0/1, /PDTACK pulled up (full speed, like SNK carts).

## Layout and routing (final 2026-10-01)
Bus row (y 114) directly above its fingers: ADPCM-A (U7 U6 U10) over x 75–93, ADPCM-B (U11 U8 U9, 1.5–2 mm apart) over x 95–135,
P glue (U13 U15 U16) over the strobes, **U4 at x 172 so every data pin is left of the right shell-post hole keep-out**, U5 right of it.
Glue row y 100.5 (U14 U18 JP1 U12 U17), LDO at (141, 90), flash row **y 48** (U2 x 78, U3 x 118, U1 x 190): the fan-outs need ~60 mm.
4 test points only (GND, 3V3, 5V, /PROG): through-hole pads cost routing space on both layers.
Rules: 0.15 mm tracks/clearance (0.127 allowed by the board rules), 0.5/0.3 mm vias (JLCPCB standard, no surcharge), power 0.3 mm.
Flow: `gen_pboard.py` (FLASH_Y, U2_X, U3_X env) → `route_pboard.py 30 <placements…>` (one Freerouting job per placement, ranked by
KiCad DRC open count, not Freerouting's own count) → `finish_v2.sh` (removes one GND bar Freerouting drew across U6's pins, routes SDRAD7,
GND pours on both layers above the tab + 6 mm stitching vias, reconnects U6 pin 39, silkscreen tidy, refill + DRC) → `export_pboard.py`.
Result: **0 DRC errors, 0 unconnected**, 430-ish vias, 53 SMD placements all on top. Warnings left: 1 reference over a pad (U15), 6 "footprint
differs from library" on the fingers/test points (built from the proven blank board, expected).
Lessons: flash too close to the bus row (y 76) left 14–15 open; the hole keep-out in the D-bus fan-out cost several nets; tight chip
spacing where SDxAD0-7 feed three chips; pre-stitching every power pin with a via made routing worse (20+ open); Freerouting's
"unrouted" figure disagreed with DRC by up to 19 — always rank by DRC.

## v3: shared sample flash (done 2026-10-01)
Timing source: neogeodev wiki "YM2610 ADPCM bus": ADPCM-A read = 6 × 1.5 µs (/SDROE low 1.5 µs, data latched on its rising edge),
ADPCM-B read = 6 × 250 ns (/SDPOE low 250 ns), and "SDROE and SDPOE go low at the same time". So B owns the flash while /SDPOE is low
(mux + 110 ns flash + 8T245 ≈ 125 ns < 250 ns), A for the rest of its window (~1.25 µs). The 74LVC16374 registers' tri-state outputs are the
mux: U6/U7 (A) and U8/U9 (B) drive one V_ADR bus. Break-before-make: B on = /SDPOE OR d, A on = NOT(/SDPOE AND d), d = /SDPOE through two
LVC04 gates (U18 g3-4, U17 g3, U14 g2, U18 g5). Flash /OE = /SDROE AND /SDPOE. Writes only through the A path (/ROMOEU), /SDPOE held high.
Simulation: 14/14, incl. both strobes falling together and the frozen-d mid-transition state; 4 of 5 planted mutations caught (the 5th is
equivalent in every legal state). Layout: data bus D0-D15 pre-routed as a locked planar staircase (`preroute_dbus.py`, horizontals y 135.25-140.5,
below the right hole keep-out), no passives in any bus chip's finger corridor, U2 at (97, 50), U1 at (203, 102) next to U4/U5. First Freerouting
run with those two rules: 0 open, 0 errors (`finish_v3.sh`: pour + stitching + silkscreen, DRC 0/0). 56 parts, 51 placements, parts $19.97/board @5.
Compatibility: 140/175 MAME parents; lost vs v2 = 13 early games with a separate ADPCM-B ROM (NAM-1975, Magician Lord, Cyber-Lip, …).
**Before ordering: capture /SDROE, /SDPOE, SDRMPX, SDPMPX, CLK68K on a real MVS (KOF96 playing) to confirm the wiki timing.**

## Historical note: the v3 idea as first written
SNK's cartridge "PCM" chip lets ADPCM-A and ADPCM-B share the same V ROMs: it takes SDRMPX, SDPMPX, /SDROE, /SDPOE and "takes advantage
of the predictable access relation between the ADPCM-A and ADPCM-B accesses" with a timing counter clocked by 68KCLKB (neogeodev wiki,
PCM page). Doing the same here would save one S29GL128P (~$7.36) but needs a CPLD or muxes + counter, and the real A/B access timing,
which is not documented: capture it with a logic analyzer (or the programmer board) on a real MVS first. v2 keeps two chips: no timing assumptions.

## Cost-down 2026-10-01 (no layout change)
U4/U5 → Nexperia 74LVC16245ADGG (C548160), U13/U14 → Nexperia 74LVC08APW (C6053): pin tables read from the Nexperia datasheets
(16245 numbers bits from 0, same physical pins; DIR high = A→B as on TI), land patterns compared pad by pad with the routed footprints
(0.000 mm difference), so the routed board is unchanged (DRC 0/0). 74LVC04 stays TI (the Nexperia listing had 3 in stock).
Parts: $17.94/board @5, $14.25 @30, $12.89 @100. Order-time options: Global Standard shipping ($12.49 for 5 vs DHL $32.26),
Economic assembly (setup $8.18 + stencil $1.53; extended-part fee unpublished, ~$3 × 9 assumed), account coupons.
