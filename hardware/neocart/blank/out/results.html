Deliberate differences from the diag boards

  • ROMWAIT, PWAIT0, PWAIT1, PDTACK (PROG B25 to B28): the diag board ties them to VCC. Here they are plain test points, so the real MVS behaviour can be measured.
  • SYSTEMB (pin 42 on both boards): the diag boards call it SLOTCS. Same slot-select signal; the wiki and jamma-nation-x name is used.
  • 2H1 (CHA A17) is labelled SA3 on the diag board; SDRD1 (CHA B48) is a typo (SRDR1) there; FIX0 to FIX7 are FIXD0 to FIXD7 there; 68KCLKB is 68KCLB there.

Labelling rule

The two test-point rows are physical, so the naming follows the rows on both faces: the upper row is the B side and is named above it, the lower row is the A side and is named below it, in the band above the gold fingers. Power pins carry one name. Where a B name would run into a shell-post hole (three columns per board, pins 47 to 49) it stays above its row in a condensed 0.7 × 0.55 mm face that ends before the hole. A legend line states the rule on each face.

Label sizes: 0.8 mm above the rows, 0.7 mm in the band and for the three condensed names, the size the manufactured diag board uses for every label. JLCPCB's guideline is 0.8 mm; the band cannot hold 0.8 mm text for seven-letter names.

What this board proves, and what it does not

Proves

  • the generator reproduces a manufactured outline to the millimetre
  • 120 + 120 pin names against two sources, on both faces
  • the gerber, drill and order pipeline end to end, gold fingers included
  • physical fit in a donor shell, once the boards arrive
  • a bus probe you keep: every MVS signal on a named 1.0 mm pad

Does not prove

  • part sourcing and JLCPCB assembly orientation
  • level shifting, flash timing, the RP2350 programmer
  • QMTech header orientation (no FPGA footprint on purpose)

Those belong to the next board, the P-ROM-only cart.
