The Fighter Lab in five steps

One page for every fighter: open the cast, tap a face. His page has five tabs: Info, Dictionary (every animation of his source game), Workshop, Assembly and Review.

1. Unlock a move (Workshop)

  1. Open the Workshop tab. Each special has a card: UNLOCKED ones have an id such as S-004 (a special) or T-001 (a throw); LOCKED ones are not decoded yet.
  2. On a locked card, say or type what you want in its thread (the microphone works) and send it. That message goes to Claude: decoding a move costs tokens, so this is the only place that talks to Claude.
  3. When it is done the card shows its new id and its knobs.

2. Assemble (Assembly)

  1. Open the Assembly tab. Each input has a slot that starts with what the game plays now.
  2. Pick animation puts any animation or unlocked piece in the slot; + add after plays another one right after it; Clear empties it; Back to now returns to the game's own move.
  3. Under an unlocked special: its knobs (speed, rise, hits, damage...). A changed knob is bold, says "changed from N" and has Back to default.
  4. Chain combo: the A presses, then Chain timing: for each press its hit-stop (how long both fighters freeze on contact) and its startup / active / recovery frames (half to double the original). Same marks: bold when changed, Back to default, Reset the chain timing.
  5. Everything saves by itself as you go. A question about a slot: Ask in the Workshop.

3. Try it (Try in game)

  1. ▶ Try this sheet in game opens the game in the page with this fighter against a dummy.
  2. Play with the keys (W A S D stick, U = A, I = B jump, O = C special, P = D fury) or a gamepad or the touch pad. Enter = the practice menu.

4. Send it to the Player

  • It is automatic: about two seconds after a change, the Assembly becomes his live config (admin accounts only). ⇪ Send to Player sends it again by hand.
  • On the phone: Player ☰ → Character lab → his face. The light says NEW CONFIG, then APPLIED once he is standing still. Assembly on that strip opens this same page beside the game.
  • Made a mistake? The Live config line has History and Revert: nothing is ever lost.

5. Ship it to the game

  • When the whole sheet is right: Ship to game at the bottom of the Assembly (admin).
  • No Claude involved: the compiler links every pick, knob and timing, builds the game, plays each slot in the emulator and publishes. If anything does not fit, it refuses and says why; the game stays as it was.
Not about one fighter (stages, enemies, the select screen, feedback replays, decisions, sounds)? Those are the Game tools.
